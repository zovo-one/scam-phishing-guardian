How Guardian keeps watch

Sites you visit are checked on your device against public threat lists (Phishing.Database, URLhaus) and local look-alike heuristics. No browsing history and no page content ever leaves this device. The optional certificate-age check sends only a bare domain name, never a full URL, to the public crt.sh certificate log, and can be turned off below.

  • Guardian warns only when several warning signs agree, and it never blocks a site. The choice is always yours.
  • Threat data comes from public lists: Phishing.Database (MIT) and URLhaus (abuse.ch).
